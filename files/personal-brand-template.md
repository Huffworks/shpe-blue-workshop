## Slide rules

Every deck follows these eight rules, whether I build it by hand or an assistant builds it.

1. Slides are widescreen 16:9 on a white background. Every content slide has a [primary color name] title bar across the top, with the title set in white [heading font].
2. The slide title is a full sentence that states the takeaway, not a topic label.
3. A slide has at most five bullets, each bullet has at most twelve words, and bullets are set in [body font].
4. Every number carries its unit and names its source document. A number that is not in the source is flagged as unsupported, never invented.
5. [Accent color name] appears at most once per slide, reserved for the one number or phrase that matters most.
6. There is no clip art, no stock photography and no decorative icons. Diagrams use only my three colors, plus the Light color for fills and backgrounds.
7. Every slide except the title slide has a footer in [secondary color name], with "[your name] | [your team, club or class]" on the left and the slide number on the right.
8. A 5-slide briefing runs in this order. Slide 1 is the title slide: its title states the takeaway and it has no bullets. Slide 2 is the evidence, with each measured number beside its limit. Slide 3 is the cause or main driver, marked as confirmed or likely. Slide 4 is what is still open, including any claim in the source that its own data does not support. Slide 5 is the ask with the next steps, and its title is a full sentence naming the decision or action requested. If my instructions say an audience wants the ask first, put the ask in the slide 1 title, keep the takeaway as the slide 1 subtitle, and make slide 5 the next steps; slides 2 to 4 stay in order.
